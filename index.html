Directions & How It Works

The Secret Protocol

From the first discreet transmission to the final applause, our staging process is seamless and theatrical.

01

The Inquiry

You submit your event vision or call our hotline. We align on guest count, venue acoustics, and your desired degree of mystery.

02

Bespoke Scripting

Our playwrights and artisans custom-craft the plot, fabricate customized cipher boxes, and assign character roles to guests.

03

The Infiltration

Professional actors in period attire blend into your party as guests, eccentric hosts, or suspects, kicking off live interactive drama.

04

The Revelation

The climax unfolds. Locks open, secrets are unveiled, awards and certificates are presented, and an unforgettable memory is sealed.
